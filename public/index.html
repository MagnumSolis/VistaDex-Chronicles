How it works

A four-stage pipeline between your idea and your belief in it.

Most screeners stop at "looks promising." VistaDex is built to attack the idea — with evidence, historical failure data, and an adversarial model — before a human reviewer forms an opinion.

01

Extract

An LLM decomposes the idea into atomic, falsifiable claims — novelty, market size, defensibility. No claim, no screen.

02

Verify

Each claim is checked against live web retrieval. Confirmed, contradicted, disputed, or unverified — never guessed.

03

Match

The full idea is embedded and matched against failed, pivoted, and quietly dead startups from the graveyard database.

04

Object

An adversarial Objector argues against the system's own conclusion. You see the case against, not just the verdict.

The report

Not a score. A research brief on your idea's survival odds.

Every report opens with a verdict and its evidence trail: claim-by-claim verification with cited sources, graveyard matches with similarity scores, an adversarial objection pass, and a "verify yourself" checklist of what the system could not confirm.

Built for accelerator screening days, university innovation programs, and VC pre-diligence — anywhere ideas are judged before they're funded.

Human–AI interaction research

Every screen is also a research artifact.

VistaDex is a live study in trust calibration: we measure confidence before and after the report, agreeableness with individual claims and objections, whether reviewers actually check the cited evidence, and satisfaction with the report itself.

Consent-first and pseudonymous by design — no personal data is ever recorded, and nothing is logged before users opt in. Built at BITS Pilani for the F364 Human–AI Interaction course.
